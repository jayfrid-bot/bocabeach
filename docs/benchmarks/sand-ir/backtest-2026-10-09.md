## Verdict: no retune yet — but the wind term is the suspect

- Every stronger-wind candidate helps the total (MAE 7.0 → 6.3 for ÷40/floor 0.4 or ÷30/floor 0.3), and the grid's best (MAE 5.8) also reaches for a stronger wind term. The old "weaker wind (÷90)" candidate is now clearly worse.
- But today is the ONLY reading above 10 mph. Even the strongest plausible wind term (÷30, floor 0.3) leaves today at about +7, so wind alone does not close the gap. Candidates: sand damp from humid onshore air / overnight dew (the model only damps for rain in the last 3 h), and the surface-vs-IR difference on a wind-stirred surface.
- Retuning a 16-reading model on one point is how the "stored heat" theory got refuted last week. Hold.
