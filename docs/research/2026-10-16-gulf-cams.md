# Gulf coast and Keys cams: five new beaches, twelve new cams (2026-10-16)

## Bottom line

On 2026-10-16 the owner decided to add every usable Florida cam now. Each cam carries a credit line that names its owner and a link to the owner's page. If an owner objects, the cam comes down the same day (see "Taking a cam down").

- **Five beaches are new:** Siesta Key, Anna Maria Island, Panama City Beach, Key West and Marathon.
- **Ten cams are new.** Two of them join Fort Myers Beach, which already existed. That makes twelve cams on six beaches.
- **Nothing is deployed.** The branch is `cams-gulf`. Not merged. The home server is untouched.
- **Courier load is held flat.** The ten new cams are "slow" cams: five pulls a day each, at the cadence of the slowest existing cams. See "YouTube load".

Source research: `2026-10-15-west-facing-cams.md` (cam table) and `2026-10-16-cam-owner-contacts.md` (owners and emails).

Notes on the picks:

- All five were run through `lib/resolve` (tide stations, NDBC stations, NWS surf zone) and then checked by hand against the live NOAA and NWS feeds.
- **Siesta Key and Anna Maria Island use buoy 42098 alone.** The resolver offered Venice (VENF1) and Port Manatee (PMAF1). VENF1 reports no water temperature today. PMAF1 sits inside Tampa Bay.
- **Panama City Beach has no wave buoy on purpose,** as Fort Myers Beach has. The nearest wave station, 42039, is about 98 miles south in deep water. Surf is model-sourced and labeled.
- **Key West and Marathon have no surf zone.** NWS Key West issues no Surf Zone Forecast. The rip-risk card has nothing to read there.
- **Marathon has no Healthy Beaches site.** The only Marathon site, Sombrero Beach, is on the Atlantic side.
- **Marathon's rip-current model point is on the Atlantic shore** (2.6 km away, `config/nwpsRip.ts`, generated). The Sunset Grille view is on Florida Bay. Treat the rip card on that beach as Atlantic-side information.
- `coastNormalDeg` and `coast` stay unset, as for the first two Gulf beaches.
- The Panama City Beach pin is near the two ENCO cams. Sharky's is about four miles north-west.

## YouTube load

YouTube starts its bot check at about 150 yt-dlp calls in 15 minutes from one address. The brief said: do not raise the courier's pull rate.

**Before this change** the Mac courier pulled every cam on every run: 7 pulls at :07 each daylight hour, and 7 pulls every 10 minutes inside a golden window. Adding ten cams to that loop would have raised the rate by 143%.

**What was built.** The courier script (`scripts/cam_courier_local.sh`) now knows a `:slow` tier. A slow cam is pulled:

- only on the hourly job, never on a golden run;
- only at UTC hours 10, 13, 16, 19 and 22 (the worker's own "surface hours", `3h-daylight`);
- on `--force`, every cam is pulled, as before.

All ten new cams are slow. They are also last in the worker registry, on `3h-daylight`, so the worker's tick budget cuts them first. `scripts/golden_now.py` and the golden plist did not change: slow cams never join golden runs, so adding the new beaches there would only widen the golden windows of the old cams. `scripts/cam_courier_tier_test.py` tests the tier.

**Extra pulls per hour** (estimates from the code, not measured):

| Source | Extra calls | Per hour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Courier, slow cams | 10 pulls in each of 4 to 5 hourly runs a day (40 to 50 a day) | 1.7 to 2.1 averaged over 24 h; 10 extra in a surface-hour run (17 instead of 7) |
| Sun-cam check (`scripts/sun_cam_check.py`) | about 3 `yt-dlp -J` per cam per event (one to open, a refresh every 18 s while it pulls 26 frames), 10 cams, 2 events a day: about 60 a day | about 2.5 averaged. Two bursts a day of about 30 calls over 10 to 15 minutes. A capture that comes up incomplete retries every 30 minutes and adds about 3 per retry. |
| Worker browser fallback | none from the home address | none |

Total: about 4 to 5 extra calls an hour on average. The worst 15-minute window adds about 30 calls. The old load is roughly 21 calls in 15 minutes inside a golden window, so the sum stays well under 150. A courtesy to the "courier pacing" crew: the sun-cam bursts are the larger share. A pause of a few seconds between cams there would flatten them.

The vision loop and archive read frames from the worker, not from YouTube, so they add no YouTube calls. They do add a vision-model read per new cam. The Eastern-time run window for the loop now ends about 10 minutes later in summer (Anna Maria Island sets last), pinned in `scripts/cam_seaweed_test.py`.

## Open-Meteo

The brief asked for no new Open-Meteo calls beyond what each new beach gets by default. This change adds none. The resolver runs used the local registries and the NWS API only (no geocoder). Each of the five new beaches gets the same default fetches as any beach, nothing more. Five beaches were added.

## Taking a cam down

1. Set `archive: false` on the cam in `config/locations.ts` if frames must stop at once, then run `scripts/cam_archive_remove.ts` to erase stored frames.
2. Remove the cam from `config/locations.ts`, `config/sun-cams.json`, `config/vision-cams.json`, the courier `CAMS` array and `workers/uw-frame/src/lib/schedule.ts`. Regenerate `config/archive-cams.json` with `npx vite-node -c vitest.config.ts scripts/gen_archive_cams.ts`.
3. Deploy the worker, then pull on the home server. The tests list every place that must agree.
