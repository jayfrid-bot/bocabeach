# Cam owner contacts for permission requests (research, 2026-10-16)

Source list: `2026-10-15-west-facing-cams.md` ("Needs permission" and "Recommended" tables).

Method: owner websites, press pages and public directories only. Nobody was contacted. No form was submitted. No YouTube tool was run. Every email below was seen on the page named in "Source". Where a page hides an email behind Cloudflare protection, the table says so.

## Bottom line

- Three cams have a clear, reachable owner with a named person or a published email: Fort Myers Beach (Margaritaville), Marathon (Overseas Media Group), Panama City Beach (ENCO).
- Siesta Key is a public cam. Sarasota County owns it, and Visit Sarasota County promotes it. Treat it like the two courtesy cams.
- Holmes Beach (item 3) has no identifiable owner. The re-streamer is the only name we have.
- Cruise Port Key West (item 8) has an owner name (Subsurface Media) but no published email.
- Fort Zachary Taylor (item 9): the park's own site runs a beach cam. We could not prove it is the same feed that "Florida Live" re-streams.

## Gaps and cautions

- The Visit Sarasota emails and the Margaritaville emails sit behind Cloudflare email protection on their pages. The Margaritaville addresses also appear in the page source. The Visit Sarasota addresses came from a fetch tool that decoded them; check one with a call before a mass send.
- Staff names change. Dates: Cesario (2023 article), Waichulis (2023), Burns (2024), Schrock (2018 to 2020 releases).
- The Cloudflare check on pinkshell.com, visitsarasota.com and sria-fla.com blocked direct fetches. Those pages were read through a fetch tool only.
